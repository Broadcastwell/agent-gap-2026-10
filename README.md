# The Agent Gap, October 2026

Broadcastwell's separate dated study of 70 vendors, three buyer tasks and one agent. All 210 primary sessions and all 30 preselected repeats are recorded. 159 of 209 scored primary sessions pass. One of 210 primary sessions, Pipedrive S2, has unknown EUR evidence and is unscored. The same rule leaves Pipedrive repeats S1 and S2 unscored. Found, Correct and Reached triplets agree in 27 of 28 evaluable repeat pairs among all 30 pairs. 2 pairs include unknown scores and cannot be counted as agreement or disagreement.

The agent is ChatGPT Work with Cloud browser. Incremental study spend was $0 using the existing signed-in seat. The selection is the five most-named vendors in each of the 14 categories in Absence Index release 2026-09. Ties use vendor-domain cited count and then alphabetical order. The vendor set and repeat sample were frozen before the first session. The study is single-agent and single-run: one primary run per task, with a separately reported fixed repeat sample. Every repeat began at least two hours after its primary observed end. It does not alter any Index named or cited count.

Perplexity Computer ran five sessions on two vendors before its seat showed no credits; they are not part of the study figures. Preserved receipts identify Procore (three) and Buildertrend (two). They are excluded from every count in this dataset. The owner's dated one-agent amendment is included; frozen vendor and repeat selection hashes remain unchanged.

Tasks: describe the vendor from its own site, open its pricing page and report the shown plans/prices, and reach its empty demo or sales form. Nothing is typed into or submitted on a vendor site. Correct is checked against the vendor's own site read on the same UTC day. Reached requires the requested page without a block, challenge or empty render. A pass requires Found, Correct and Reached all yes. An accurate report that no public price is shown can pass. A missing result is never silently converted to a failure.

Every tested vendor may request one re-run of its three tasks at no cost at index@broadcastwell.com. Both original and re-run results will be published with dates and reasons. No vendor is ranked or endorsed. These results do not establish product quality, overall site accessibility, the share of buyers using agents, or any promise of shortlist, position, inclusion, lead or revenue. Domain-only tasks can reach another product family; the report discloses those cases.

AGENT_GAP_DATA.csv contains 240 session rows. Blank Correct in Pipedrive primary S2 and repeat S1/S2 means unknown evidence, neither pass nor fail. The same-day official English and German locale pricing pages served USD and could neither verify nor refute the buyer's EUR claims. Receipt and screenshot columns are paths in the retained evidence package, not claims that those files are hosted in this repository. AGENT_GAP_SET.csv and AGENT_GAP_REPEAT_SET.csv are the frozen selection files. The full report includes per-task and overall 95 percent Wilson intervals, all 14 categories, every vendor and all 30 repeat pairs. The full report and method will be linked after their public URLs are verified.
